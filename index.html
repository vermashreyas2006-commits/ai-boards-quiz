Supported Boards

  • CBSE: Central Board curriculum spanning Classes 6 to 12 based on the latest NCERT learning benchmarks.
  • ICSE & ISC: CISCE curriculum emphasizing analytical depth, comprehensive science, and applied mathematics.
  • State Boards: Maharashtra, Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Uttar Pradesh, West Bengal, Kerala, Andhra Pradesh, Gujarat, and more.
📐

Question Format & Rigor

  • Four Strict Options: Every generated MCQ is validated on the backend to contain exactly 4 unique options.
  • Single Verified Answer: One definitively correct answer mapped strictly to board standards.
  • Conceptual Clarity: Detailed pedagogical explanations included for every question to strengthen concepts.
⚡

Board Exam Tips

  • Time Management: Practice with 15-minute 10-question blocks to build steady exam pacing.
  • Conceptual Mistakes: Always review incorrect answers to understand *why* the distractor was wrong.
  • NCERT Foundation: NCERT and core textbooks form the bedrock of 80%+ of board exam questions.
